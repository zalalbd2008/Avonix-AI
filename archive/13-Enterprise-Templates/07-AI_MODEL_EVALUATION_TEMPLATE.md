# Enterprise AI Model Evaluation Template

> "Every AI model should earn trust through measurable performance, responsible governance, and continuous evaluation."

---

# Purpose

This template defines the official Enterprise AI Model Evaluation format for Avonix AI.

It establishes a standardized process for evaluating AI models before deployment and throughout their operational lifecycle, ensuring technical excellence, business alignment, regulatory compliance, and responsible AI practices.

---

# Philosophy

AI model evaluation should be:

- Evidence-based
- Repeatable
- Transparent
- Risk-aware
- Human-centered
- Governance-driven
- Continuously monitored

Evaluation should measure not only model performance, but also safety, fairness, operational readiness, and long-term sustainability.

---

# Objectives

This template ensures:

- Standardized AI evaluations
- Responsible AI governance
- Business alignment
- Technical validation
- Risk identification
- Deployment readiness
- Audit readiness

---

# Scope

Applicable to:

- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- Machine Learning Models
- Classification Models
- Regression Models
- Recommendation Engines
- Vision Models
- Speech Models
- Internal AI Services
- Third-party AI APIs
- Fine-tuned Enterprise Models
