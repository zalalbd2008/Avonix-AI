# Product Metrics

> "What gets measured consistently gets improved intentionally."

---

# Purpose

This document defines the canonical product measurement framework for Avonix AI.

It establishes:

- North Star Metric
- Business metrics
- Product adoption metrics
- Customer success metrics
- AI metrics
- Operational metrics
- Quality metrics
- Metric governance

The purpose is to ensure every team evaluates success using a shared measurement framework.

---

# Measurement Philosophy

Metrics exist to improve decision-making, not to create reports.

Every metric should:

- Support a strategic objective
- Be measurable
- Be actionable
- Be understandable
- Have a clear owner
- Be reviewed regularly

Metrics without decisions are operational noise.

---

# Measurement Hierarchy

```
Vision

↓

Strategic Objectives

↓

North Star Metric

↓

Outcome Metrics

↓

Operational Metrics

↓

Diagnostic Metrics
```

Higher-level metrics explain success.

Lower-level metrics explain why.

---

# North Star Metric

## Definition

The primary measure of long-term product success.

For Avonix AI:

**Monthly Active Organizations Achieving Meaningful Value (MAOMV)**

An organization is considered successful when it actively uses core platform capabilities to accomplish measurable business outcomes.

---

# Business Metrics

Business metrics evaluate commercial performance.

Examples:

- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
- Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)
- Expansion Revenue
- Average Revenue per Organization (ARPO)
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
- Gross Revenue Retention
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR)
- Logo Churn
- Revenue Churn

---

# Customer Success Metrics

Measure whether customers become successful.

Examples:

- Time to First Value (TTFV)
- Activation Rate
- Onboarding Completion Rate
- 7-Day Adoption
- 30-Day Adoption
- Customer Health Score
- Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)

---

# Product Usage Metrics

Measure how customers use the platform.

Examples:

- Daily Active Organizations (DAO)
- Weekly Active Organizations (WAO)
- Monthly Active Organizations (MAO)
- Feature Adoption Rate
- Workspace Engagement
- Website Connection Rate
- Knowledge Base Growth
- Conversation Volume

---

# AI Metrics

Measure AI effectiveness.

Examples:

- AI Resolution Rate
- AI Recommendation Acceptance
- AI Response Accuracy
- AI Escalation Rate
- AI Knowledge Utilization
- AI Automation Success
- AI Hallucination Reports
- AI Response Time

AI quality should be evaluated continuously.

---

# Automation Metrics

Measure workflow automation performance.

Examples:

- Workflow Success Rate
- Automation Adoption
- Manual Tasks Eliminated
- Workflow Failures
- Retry Success Rate
- Automation Execution Time

---

# Operational Metrics

Measure platform reliability.

Examples:

- Availability
- Error Rate
- Mean Time to Detect (MTTD)
- Mean Time to Resolve (MTTR)
- API Latency
- Queue Processing Time
- Background Job Success
- Notification Delivery Rate

---

# Security Metrics

Measure platform trustworthiness.

Examples:

- Failed Login Rate
- Permission Violations
- Security Incidents
- Audit Completeness
- MFA Adoption
- Threat Detection Time
- Compliance Status

---

# Quality Metrics

Measure overall product quality.

Examples:

- Bug Escape Rate
- Regression Rate
- Release Stability
- Crash Rate
- Customer-Reported Issues
- Performance Score
- Accessibility Compliance

---

# Adoption Funnel

The platform should measure customer progression.

```
Sign Up

↓

Organization Created

↓

Website Connected

↓

AI Configured

↓

First Value

↓

Weekly Usage

↓

Monthly Adoption

↓

Expansion

↓

Renewal
```

Every stage should define measurable conversion targets.

---

# Metric Ownership

Each metric should have:

- Metric Owner
- Business Purpose
- Definition
- Calculation Formula
- Data Source
- Reporting Frequency
- Alert Threshold
- Executive Visibility

No metric should exist without ownership.

---

# Metric Classification

Metrics should be categorized as:

- Strategic
- Outcome
- Operational
- Diagnostic
- Experimental

This classification helps prioritize reporting and decision-making.

---

# Metric Governance

Metric definitions are version-controlled.

Changes require:

- Product review
- Analytics review
- Executive approval (where applicable)

Historical metrics should remain comparable across releases.

---

# Dashboard Principles

Executive dashboards should emphasize:

- Trends over snapshots
- Outcomes over activity
- Exceptions over averages
- Actionable insights over raw data
