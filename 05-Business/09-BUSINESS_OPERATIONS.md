# Business Operations

> "Operational excellence is achieved when every team executes consistently, every process is measurable, and every improvement compounds over time."

---

# Purpose

This document defines the canonical Business Operations architecture for Avonix AI.

It establishes:

- Operations philosophy
- Operating model
- Functional operations
- Planning framework
- Operational controls
- Business continuity
- Performance management
- Governance

This document serves as the authoritative reference for how Avonix AI operates as an organization.

---

# Operations Philosophy

Business Operations should be:

- Repeatable
- Scalable
- Transparent
- Measurable
- Efficient
- Continuously improving

Operations exist to enable every team to execute consistently.

---

# Strategic Objectives

Business Operations should:

- Improve organizational efficiency
- Standardize execution
- Reduce operational risk
- Support sustainable growth
- Improve decision quality
- Increase cross-functional collaboration

---

# Operating Model

Business execution follows a continuous operating cycle.

```
Strategy

↓

Planning

↓

Prioritization

↓

Execution

↓

Monitoring

↓

Measurement

↓

Review

↓

Optimization

↓

Continuous Improvement
```

Every operational activity should align with this lifecycle.

- Escalations

# Operational Controls

Operational controls should include:

- Approval workflows
- Segregation of responsibilities
- Documentation standards
- Change management
- Risk controls
- Audit trails

Controls should balance governance with execution speed.

---

# Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

Every critical business process should define:

- Purpose
- Scope
- Inputs
- Outputs
- Responsibilities
- Workflow
- Success criteria
- Exception handling

SOPs should be version-controlled.

---

# Cross-Functional Collaboration

Business Operations should coordinate:

- Executive Leadership
- Product
- Engineering
- Design
- AI
- Sales
- Marketing
- Customer Success
- Finance
- Legal

Shared accountability improves execution quality.

---

# Decision Framework

Operational decisions should define:

- Decision owner
- Stakeholders
- Required approvals
- Supporting data
- Risk assessment
- Success criteria

Decision authority should be explicit.

---

# Resource Management

Business resources should include:

- Budget
- Personnel
- Technology
- Infrastructure
- Vendor capacity
- AI resources

Resource allocation should reflect strategic priorities.

---

# Change Management

Operational change should follow:

```
Proposal

↓

Impact Assessment

↓

Approval

↓

Communication

↓

Implementation

↓

Monitoring

↓

Validation

↓

Documentation
```

Every significant change should be documented.

---

# Business Continuity

Operational resilience should include:

- Continuity planning
- Disaster recovery coordination
- Incident communication
- Recovery procedures
- Operational fallback processes

Business continuity should be regularly reviewed.

---

# Knowledge Management

Operational knowledge should include:

- Policies
- SOPs
- Playbooks
- Templates
- Decision records
- Training materials

Knowledge should remain centralized and searchable.

---

# Performance Management

Operational performance should monitor:

- SLA compliance
- OKR achievement
- Process efficiency
- Cycle time
- Resource utilization
- Forecast accuracy
- Operational cost
- Customer impact

Performance measurement should drive improvement.

---

# Operational Analytics

Executive dashboards should report:

- Revenue performance
- Customer metrics
- Operational efficiency
- Product delivery
- AI usage
- Financial performance
- Organizational health

Analytics should support executive decision-making.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Improvement initiatives should be based on:

- KPI trends
- Customer feedback
- Employee feedback
- Audit findings
- Process reviews
- Innovation opportunities

Improvement should become part of normal operations.

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Departmental silos
- Undefined ownership
- Manual processes without justification
- Duplicate workflows
- Outdated SOPs
- Reactive operations

Operational maturity requires discipline and consistency.

---

# Business Operations Review Checklist

Every operational initiative should answer:

- Are responsibilities clearly defined?
- Are SOPs documented?
- Are controls appropriate?
- Are KPIs measurable?
- Are risks understood?
- Are dependencies identified?
- Is change management complete?
- Is continuous improvement planned?

---

# Governance

Business Operations should maintain:

- Process registry
- SOP repository
- OKR registry
- KPI dashboards
- Decision log
- Change log
- Risk register
- Operational review calendar

Governance ensures consistent execution across the organization.
