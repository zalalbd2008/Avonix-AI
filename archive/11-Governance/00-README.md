# Governance

> "Architecture defines direction. Governance ensures the direction is consistently followed."

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Governance layer for Avonix AI.

The Governance layer establishes the enterprise-wide policies, oversight mechanisms, accountability model, review processes, and decision authorities that ensure the platform continues to evolve in a secure, compliant, reliable, and strategically aligned manner.

Governance transforms standards into organizational discipline.

---

# Philosophy

Governance should be:

- Transparent
- Accountable
- Risk-aware
- Business-aligned
- Evidence-based
- Consistent
- Continuously improving

Governance should enable innovation while protecting long-term platform integrity.

---

# Objectives

This layer should ensure:

- Consistent architectural decisions
- Technology standardization
- Responsible AI governance
- Security oversight
- Regulatory compliance
- Risk management
- Operational accountability
- Continuous policy evolution

# Governance Principles

Every governance decision should promote:

- Strategic alignment
- Business value
- Technical consistency
- Measurable outcomes
- Responsible ownership
- Controlled change
- Continuous improvement

Governance exists to support sustainable platform evolution rather than unnecessary bureaucracy.

---

# Decision Hierarchy

Governance decisions should follow a defined hierarchy:

1. Vision and Business Strategy
2. Enterprise Architecture
3. Organizational Policies
4. Engineering Standards
5. Operational Standards
6. Team Procedures

Higher-level decisions should guide lower-level implementation.

---

# Governance Bodies

Oversight may include:

- Governance Council
- Architecture Council
- Engineering Council
- AI Council
- Security Council
- Operations Council
- Product Leadership

Each body should have clearly documented responsibilities and decision authority.

# Review Cadence

Governance should define recurring reviews for:

- Compliance

Review frequency should reflect business needs and organizational risk.
