# Business Architecture

> "A great product becomes a great company only when its business model is as intentional as its technology."

---

# Purpose

The **05-Business** layer defines the commercial architecture of Avonix AI.

It establishes how the platform creates, delivers, captures, and sustains value across customers, partners, and internal operations.

While previous layers define *what the product is* and *how it is built*, this layer defines *how the business succeeds*.

---

# Scope

This layer defines:

- Business model
- Pricing strategy
- Packaging
- Go-to-market strategy
- Sales architecture
- Customer success
- Partner ecosystem
- Marketing strategy
- Legal & compliance
- Business operations
- Business governance

---

# Guiding Principles

The business architecture should be:

- Customer-centric
- Scalable
- Sustainable
- Measurable
- Ethical
- Transparent
- Data-driven
- AI-enabled

Commercial success should never compromise user trust.

---

# Core Business Objectives

The business organization exists to:

- Deliver customer value
- Generate sustainable recurring revenue
- Build long-term customer relationships
- Enable ecosystem growth
- Maintain operational excellence
- Support continuous product innovation

---

# Success Metrics

The Business architecture should improve:

- Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
- Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- CAC Payback Period
- Gross Revenue Retention (GRR)
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR)
- Churn Rate
- Expansion Revenue
- Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)

Metrics should guide strategic decisions rather than become goals in isolation.

---

# Governance

- Product strategy

- Long-term sustainability

Business governance should encourage disciplined growth rather than short-term optimization.
