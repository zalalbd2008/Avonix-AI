# Changelog

## Purpose

This document records all significant changes made to the Avonix AI documentation, product specifications, architecture, and business decisions.

It provides a chronological history of the project and acts as the official change log for documentation.

---

# Versioning Rules

Avonix AI follows Semantic Versioning.

Format:

MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH

Example:

1.0.0

Where:

Major
Breaking architectural or product changes.

Minor
New modules, features, or documentation.

Patch
Bug fixes, typo corrections, documentation improvements.
