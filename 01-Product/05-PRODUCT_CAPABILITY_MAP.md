# Product Capability Map

> "Capabilities create customer value. Modules deliver capabilities."

# Purpose

This document maps Avonix AI platform capabilities to:

- Customer problems
- Personas
- Jobs-to-be-Done
- Product modules
- Business value
- Future product evolution

Unlike the Foundation capability taxonomy, this document focuses on product strategy and customer outcomes.

Each capability is defined by:

- Business Purpose

- Supported Jobs
