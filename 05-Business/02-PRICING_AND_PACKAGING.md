# Pricing & Packaging

> "Pricing should reflect customer value, remain transparent, and scale naturally as customers grow."

---

# Purpose

This document defines the canonical pricing and packaging architecture for Avonix AI.

It establishes:

- Pricing philosophy
- Packaging strategy
- Commercial editions
- Monetization architecture
- Billing model
- Upgrade strategy
- Promotions
- Pricing governance

This document is the authoritative source for how Avonix AI packages, prices, and monetizes its platform.

---

# Pricing Philosophy

The pricing model should be:

- Value-based
- Transparent
- Predictable
- Scalable
- Sustainable
- Easy to understand

Customers should always understand what they receive and why they pay.

---

# Pricing Objectives

The pricing strategy should:

- Maximize customer value
- Encourage adoption
- Support expansion
- Reduce purchasing friction
- Align revenue with customer success
- Enable sustainable growth

---

# Packaging Philosophy

Packages should represent customer maturity rather than arbitrary feature restrictions.

Packaging should encourage growth instead of creating unnecessary barriers.

---

# Packaging Framework

Each edition may define limits for:

- Users
- Workspaces
- AI usage
- Automation runs
- Storage
- API access
- Integrations
- Analytics
- Support level

Limits should align with customer needs rather than artificially reducing usability.

# Billing Architecture

The billing system should support:

- Monthly subscriptions
- Annual subscriptions
- Seat-based licensing
- Usage metering
- Hybrid billing
- Enterprise invoicing
- Purchase orders

Billing should remain flexible across customer segments.

---

# Licensing Model

Licensing may include:

- Named users
- Concurrent users
- Workspace licenses
- Enterprise agreements
- API licenses

The licensing model should remain simple to administer.

---

# AI Credit System

AI consumption may be managed through:

- Monthly included credits
- Usage-based credits
- Enterprise credit pools
- Overage pricing
- Credit rollover (where applicable)

Customers should always be able to monitor AI usage.

---

# Upgrade Path

Customers should be able to:

- Upgrade instantly
- Increase seats
- Purchase add-ons
- Expand AI capacity
- Unlock enterprise capabilities

Expansion should be frictionless.

---

# Downgrade Policy

Downgrades should:

- Preserve customer data
- Clearly explain feature changes
- Respect contract terms
- Avoid unexpected service disruption

Trust should remain intact during plan changes.

---

# Trial Strategy

Trials may include:

- Time-limited evaluation
- Feature-limited evaluation
- AI usage credits
- Guided onboarding

Trials should demonstrate product value quickly.

---

# Discounts & Promotions

Commercial incentives may include:

- Annual billing discounts
- Educational pricing
- Startup programs
- Nonprofit pricing
- Partner pricing
- Promotional campaigns

Discount policies should remain documented and measurable.

---

# Enterprise Commercial Terms

Enterprise agreements may include:

- Custom pricing
- Multi-year contracts
- Dedicated support
- Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
- Compliance commitments
- Procurement requirements

Enterprise terms should remain governed.

---

# Taxes & Currency

The billing platform should support:

- Multiple currencies
- Regional taxation
- VAT
- GST
- Sales tax
- Tax-exempt customers

Compliance should be automated wherever possible.

---

# Customer Communication

Pricing communication should be:

- Honest
- Clear
- Predictable
- Versioned

Customers should receive advance notice of material pricing changes.

---

# Success Metrics

Pricing performance should be evaluated through:

- Conversion rate
- Free-to-paid conversion
- Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)
- Average Contract Value (ACV)
- Annual Contract Value (ACV)
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR)
- Expansion revenue
- Churn
- Discount utilization

Pricing decisions should be evidence-driven.

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Hidden fees
- Confusing feature limits
- Complex licensing rules
- Unexpected overage charges
- Inconsistent regional pricing
- Frequent pricing changes

Pricing complexity reduces customer trust.

---

# Pricing Review Checklist

Every pricing revision should answer:

- Does it increase customer value?
- Is pricing transparent?
- Is packaging easy to understand?
- Are upgrade paths clear?
- Are billing rules consistent?
- Is international support considered?
- Is pricing operationally scalable?
- Is the business impact measurable?

---

# Governance

The pricing program should maintain:

- Pricing registry
- Plan catalog
- Feature matrix
- Version history
- Approval records
- Customer communication log
- Commercial analytics

Pricing governance ensures long-term commercial consistency.
