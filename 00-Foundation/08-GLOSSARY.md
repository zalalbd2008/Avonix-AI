# Glossary

> "A shared vocabulary eliminates misunderstanding."

---

# Purpose

This glossary defines the official terminology used throughout the Avonix AI platform.

Every document, API, user interface, support article, and AI prompt should use these terms consistently.

If a term appears here, this definition is considered canonical.

---

# General Rules

- Use one term for one meaning.
- Avoid synonyms in product documentation.
- Prefer business language over technical jargon.
- Use consistent capitalization.
- Do not redefine glossary terms elsewhere.

---

# A

## AI Agent

An intelligent software worker that performs tasks, answers questions, automates workflows, or assists users within a defined scope.

---

## Analytics

Measurements, reports, trends, and insights generated from platform activity.

---

## Audit Log

An immutable historical record of important platform events used for accountability, compliance, and investigations.

---

## Automation

A rule-based workflow that performs actions automatically in response to events or conditions.

---

# C

## Contact

An identified person associated with an Organization.

A Contact may or may not become a Lead.

---

## Conversation

A communication session between a Visitor (or Contact) and an AI Agent, Live Agent, or Voice Agent.

---

## Confidence Score

A measure indicating how certain the AI is about a generated response.

Low confidence should result in clarification or escalation rather than guessing.

---

# D

## Dashboard

A high-level view of operational information designed to support decision-making.

Dashboards summarize information rather than expose raw data.

---

## Document

A file or structured content source that contributes to the platform's Knowledge.

---

# E

## Event

A recorded occurrence within the platform.

Events may trigger automation, notifications, analytics, or audit records.

---

# F

## Form

A structured interface used to collect information from Visitors or Users.

Forms may trigger automation and generate Leads.

---

# H

## Health Score

A composite indicator representing the operational condition of a Website.

It combines multiple dimensions such as security, performance, SEO, backups, availability, and accessibility.

---

# K

## Knowledge

Verified information available to AI for answering questions and supporting decisions.

Knowledge may originate from websites, documents, FAQs, manuals, or other approved sources.

---

# L

## Lead

A Contact demonstrating meaningful business intent.

Leads enter sales, support, or follow-up workflows.

---

# M

## Module

A self-contained business capability with clearly defined ownership.

Modules collaborate through shared platform services and events.

---

# N

## Notification

A message informing users about important events requiring awareness or action.

---

# O

## Organization

An independent company or business operating within the Avonix AI platform.

Organizations are isolated from one another.

---

# P

## Platform

The complete Avonix AI ecosystem providing shared infrastructure, services, governance, and business capabilities.

---

## Permission

Authorization allowing a User or Service Account to perform specific actions.

Permissions are evaluated independently from authentication.

---

## Popup

A contextual interface element displayed in response to visitor behavior or configured rules.

---

# R

## Role

A collection of permissions assigned to Users or Service Accounts.

---

# S

## Service Account

A non-human identity representing applications, AI agents, automation workflows, or integrations.

---

## Shared Service

A reusable platform capability available to multiple modules.

Examples include Search, Audit Logging, Authentication, and Notifications.

---

## Workspace

The operational environment for a single Website.

Users perform daily work inside a Workspace.

---

# U

## User

A human identity authorized to access one or more Organizations.

Users interact with the platform according to assigned permissions.

---

# V

## Visitor

A person interacting with a Website before identification.

Visitors may browse content, chat with AI, submit forms, or become Contacts and Leads.

---

# W

## Website

A connected digital property managed through the Avonix AI platform.

Each Website belongs to exactly one Organization.

---

# Terminology Guidelines

Always prefer:

- Website (not Site)
- Organization (not Company or Client)
- Workspace (not Project)
- AI Agent (not Bot)
- Conversation (not Chat Session)
- Lead (not Inquiry)
- Health Score (not Website Rating)

Consistent terminology improves usability, documentation quality, API design, and communication across teams.
