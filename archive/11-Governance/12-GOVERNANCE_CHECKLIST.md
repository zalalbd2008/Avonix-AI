# Enterprise Governance Readiness Checklist

> "Governance is complete only when policies, people, processes, controls, evidence, and accountability operate together."

---

# Purpose

This document defines the canonical Enterprise Governance Readiness Checklist for Avonix AI.

It provides the final governance validation framework used to determine whether enterprise governance is complete, operational, measurable, and sustainable across all business and technology domains.

---

# Philosophy

Governance readiness should be:

- Complete
- Consistent
- Accountable
- Evidence-based
- Risk-aware
- Audit-ready
- Continuously validated

Governance readiness represents organizational capability rather than documentation completeness alone.

---

# Objectives

This checklist should ensure:

- Enterprise governance completeness
- Organizational accountability
- Governance consistency
- Policy effectiveness
- Audit readiness
- Continuous governance improvement
- Executive confidence

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Enterprise governance
- Architecture governance
- Technology governance
- Data governance
- AI governance
- Security governance
- Risk governance
- Compliance governance
- Audit governance
- Operational governance

---

# Governance Structure Readiness

Verify:

- Governance councils established
- Decision hierarchy documented
- Governance charters approved
- Executive sponsorship assigned
- Reporting structure defined

---

# Architecture Governance Readiness

Verify:

- Architecture principles approved
- Review board operational
- ADR governance implemented
- Architecture exceptions governed
- Technical debt oversight established

---

# Technology Governance Readiness

Verify:

- Technology portfolio maintained
- Lifecycle governance implemented
- Version governance established
- EOL process defined
- Technology review process operational

---

# Data Governance Readiness

Verify:

- Data ownership assigned
- Classification framework implemented
- Data catalog available
- Metadata governance operational
- Data quality governance established

---

# AI Governance Readiness

Verify:

- AI ownership documented
- Responsible AI principles adopted
- Model governance implemented
- Prompt governance operational
- AI monitoring established

---

# Security Governance Readiness

- Security policies approved
- Identity governance operational
- Secrets governance implemented
- Vulnerability governance active
- Security review process established

# Risk Governance Readiness

Verify:

- Enterprise risk register maintained
- Risk ownership assigned
- Risk appetite approved
- Escalation framework operational
- Risk reporting active

---

# Compliance Governance Readiness

- Compliance framework documented
- Regulatory obligations mapped

- Evidence repository available
- Corrective action process operational

# Policy Lifecycle Readiness

Verify:

- Policy register maintained
- Version control implemented
- Review cadence defined
- Communication process operational
- Retirement process documented

# Audit Readiness

Verify:

- Audit framework approved
- Audit plan maintained
- Evidence collection operational
- Findings management established
- Verification process documented

---

# Governance Roles Readiness

Verify:

- Governance roles assigned
- Council memberships confirmed
- RACI matrices completed
- Delegation records maintained

# Governance Metrics Readiness

Verify:

- KPI catalog approved
- Metric ownership assigned
- Executive dashboards available
- Reporting cadence established
- Governance scorecards operational

---

# Cross-Layer Alignment

Verify alignment between:

- Foundation
- Product
- Platform
- Engineering
- Design
- Business
- AI
- Decisions
- Reference Architectures
- Implementation Standards
- Operations
- Governance

Enterprise governance should provide coherent oversight across every repository layer.

---

# Documentation Readiness

Verify:

- Policies complete
- Standards complete
- Decision records maintained
- Governance documentation current
- Evidence repository complete

Documentation should accurately reflect organizational practice.

---

# Executive Readiness

Verify:

- Executive approvals complete
- Governance funding confirmed
- Strategic alignment validated
- Organizational accountability established
- Governance review schedule approved

Enterprise governance should be fully supported by executive leadership.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Verify:

- Governance reviews scheduled
- KPI reviews operational
- Audit feedback integrated
- Lessons learned documented
- Improvement initiatives tracked

Governance maturity should increase through continuous evaluation and refinement.

---

# Final Governance Approval

Enterprise governance should be approved only when:

- Governance domains are operational
- Organizational responsibilities are assigned
- Policies are effective
- Risks are governed
- Compliance is sustainable
- Audit readiness is demonstrated
- Executive leadership confirms organizational readiness

---

# Governance

Final governance readiness requires:

- Enterprise Governance Council approval
- Executive Sponsor approval
- Architecture Council approval
- Security Council approval
- Risk Council approval
- Compliance Council approval
- Audit Council approval

Enterprise governance should become the authoritative framework for organizational oversight.
