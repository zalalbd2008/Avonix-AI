# Enterprise Risk Register Template

> "A risk that is visible, understood, and owned is far more manageable than a risk that remains undocumented."

---

# Purpose

This template provides the official Enterprise Risk Register format for Avonix AI.

It standardizes how business, technology, AI, operational, security, legal, compliance, and strategic risks are identified, evaluated, managed, monitored, and retired throughout their lifecycle.

---

# Philosophy

Risk management should be:

- Proactive
- Evidence-based
- Transparent
- Measurable
- Continuously monitored
- Governance-driven
- Business-aligned

Risk registers should support decision-making rather than become static documentation.

---

# Objectives

This template ensures:

- Enterprise-wide risk visibility
- Consistent risk documentation
- Ownership accountability
- Governance alignment
- Audit readiness
- Continuous monitoring
- Informed business decisions

---

# Scope

Applicable to risks involving:

- Enterprise Strategy
- Product
- Engineering
- AI
- Security
- Infrastructure
- Cloud
- Vendors
- Legal
- Compliance
- Finance
- Operations

# Executive Summary

Provide a concise overview covering:

- Risk overview
- Business significance
- Current exposure
- Expected outcome

---

# Business Context

Describe:

- Business objective
- Related initiative
- Operational environment
- Strategic importance

---

# Risk Description

Document:

- What could happen
- Why it matters
- Potential business consequences

The description should clearly explain the risk event.

---

# Risk Category

Select one or more categories:

- Strategic
- Operational
- Financial
- Technology
- Infrastructure
- AI
- Security
- Privacy
- Compliance
- Legal
- Vendor
- Third-Party
- Reputation

---

# Risk Classification

Record:

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Likelihood | Rare / Unlikely / Possible / Likely / Almost Certain |
| Impact | Low / Moderate / High / Severe |
| Overall Risk Rating | Low / Medium / High / Critical |

---

# Business Impact Assessment

Evaluate impact on:

- Customers
- Revenue
- Operations
- Security
- AI Services
- Compliance
- Reputation
- Availability

---

# Root Cause

Describe:

- Primary cause
- Contributing factors
- Trigger conditions

---

# Existing Controls

Document current controls including:

- Policies
- Technical safeguards
- Operational procedures
- Monitoring controls
- Governance controls

---

# Control Effectiveness

Assess:

- Effective
- Partially Effective
- Ineffective
- Unknown

Include supporting evidence where available.

---

# Mitigation Plan

Document:

- Planned actions
- Responsible owner
- Target completion date
- Expected outcome

Mitigation activities should reduce either likelihood, impact, or both.

---

# Contingency Plan

Describe:

- Response actions
- Recovery approach
- Escalation path
- Business continuity measures

---

# Residual Risk

After mitigation record:

- Remaining likelihood
- Remaining impact
- Residual risk rating
- Acceptance rationale

Residual risk should be formally reviewed before acceptance.

---

# Risk Acceptance

If accepted document:

- Acceptance decision
- Business justification
- Approval authority
- Acceptance period
- Review date

Accepted risks should remain visible until closure.

---

# Escalation Criteria

Escalate when:

- Risk rating increases
- Control failure occurs
- Business impact changes
- Regulatory obligations change
- Executive attention becomes necessary

---

# Monitoring

Monitor:

- Control performance
- KPI trends
- KRI (Key Risk Indicator) trends
- Incident history
- Audit findings
- Customer impact

Monitoring should continue until the risk is closed.

---

# Related Initiatives

Reference:

# Review Schedule

Record:

- Review frequency
- Next review date
- Review owner
- Closure criteria
