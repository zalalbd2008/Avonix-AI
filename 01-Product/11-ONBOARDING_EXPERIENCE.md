# Onboarding Experience

> "Successful onboarding is not about completing setup. It is about helping customers achieve their first meaningful outcome."

---

# Purpose

This document defines the onboarding and product activation strategy for Avonix AI.

It establishes:

- Activation philosophy
- Progressive onboarding stages
- Persona-specific journeys
- AI-assisted onboarding
- Success milestones
- Adoption measurements

This document focuses on product adoption rather than interface implementation.

---

# Activation Philosophy

The objective of onboarding is to reduce Time to First Value (TTFV).

Every onboarding step should help customers reach operational success faster.

Good onboarding should:

- Reduce uncertainty
- Minimize manual configuration
- Personalize the experience
- Build confidence
- Encourage continued adoption

---

# Core Principles

The onboarding experience should be:

- Progressive
- Context-aware
- Persona-driven
- AI-assisted
- Recoverable
- Measurable

Users should never be forced to configure features they do not need immediately.

---

# Activation Lifecycle

```
Discover

↓

Configure

↓

Activate

↓

Validate

↓

Adopt

↓

Expand

↓

Optimize
```

Each stage has clear objectives and measurable outcomes.

---

# Stage 1 — Discover

## Objective

Help users understand the platform and define their goals.

### Activities

- Welcome experience
- Product introduction
- Organization profile
- Use-case selection
- Industry selection

### Success Criteria

- Organization created
- Initial goals identified

---

# Stage 2 — Configure

## Objective

Prepare the workspace for productive use.

### Activities

- Connect website
- Configure AI
- Invite team members
- Set organization preferences
- Enable integrations

### Success Criteria

- Core configuration complete

---

# Stage 3 — Activate

## Objective

Deliver the customer's first operational success.

### Examples

- AI answers its first visitor
- First website health scan completes
- First workflow executes
- First lead captured

### Success Criteria

At least one meaningful business outcome achieved.

---

# Stage 4 — Validate

## Objective

Confirm that the platform is operating correctly.

### Activities

- AI verification
- Integration testing
- Notification testing
- Automation validation
- Health checks

### Success Criteria

All critical systems verified.

---

# Stage 5 — Adopt

## Objective

Encourage regular platform usage.

### Activities

- Daily dashboard usage
- Team collaboration
- Knowledge creation
- AI recommendations
- Analytics review

### Success Criteria

Users establish recurring workflows.

---

# Stage 6 — Expand

## Objective

Increase platform value over time.

### Activities

- Add more websites
- Enable additional modules
- Expand automation
- Increase AI usage
- Connect external systems

### Success Criteria

The organization adopts additional capabilities aligned with its needs.

---

# Stage 7 — Optimize

## Objective

Continuously improve operational performance.

### Activities

- Review KPIs
- Refine workflows
- Improve AI knowledge
- Remove operational bottlenecks
- Adopt new platform capabilities

### Success Criteria

Continuous measurable improvement.

---

# Persona-Based Journeys

## Organization Administrator

Primary Goals

- Configure organization
- Invite users
- Connect websites
- Enable AI

Primary Success

Operational readiness.

---

## Team Member

Primary Goals

- Complete assigned work
- Use AI assistance
- Collaborate effectively

Primary Success

Daily productivity.

---

## Support Agent

Primary Goals

- Respond to conversations
- Continue AI handoffs
- Resolve customer issues

Primary Success

High-quality customer support.

---

## Analyst

Primary Goals

- Review insights
- Understand trends
- Generate reports

Primary Success

Actionable business intelligence.

---

# AI-Guided Onboarding

AI should assist throughout onboarding.

Examples:

- Recommend configuration
- Explain features
- Detect missing steps
- Identify setup errors
- Suggest best practices
- Generate knowledge articles
- Recommend automations

AI recommendations should always remain explainable.

---

# Milestones

Examples include:

- Organization Created
- Website Connected
- AI Activated
- First Visitor Conversation
- First Automation
- First Report
- First Knowledge Article
- Team Invited

Milestones should be visible to customers.

---

# Progress Tracking

The platform should display:

- Completion percentage
- Remaining steps
- Recommended actions
- Estimated completion time
- Blocked tasks

Progress should update automatically.

---

# Recovery Experience

Users should always be able to:

- Pause onboarding
- Resume later
- Restart selected stages
- Skip optional tasks
- Receive contextual assistance

Progress should never be lost unintentionally.

---

# Activation Metrics

The onboarding experience should measure:

- Time to First Value (TTFV)
- Activation Rate
- Onboarding Completion Rate
- 7-Day Adoption
- 30-Day Adoption
- Feature Adoption
- AI Adoption
- Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)
