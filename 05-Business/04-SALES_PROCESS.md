# Sales Process

> "The objective of sales is not to close deals—it is to create successful customers through trusted commercial relationships."

---

# Purpose

This document defines the canonical sales execution architecture for Avonix AI.

It establishes:

- Sales philosophy
- Revenue execution model
- Sales lifecycle
- Pipeline architecture
- Sales playbooks
- Revenue Operations (RevOps)
- Enterprise sales
- Performance measurement
- Governance

This document is the authoritative reference for every commercial sales activity.

---

# Sales Philosophy

Sales should be:

- Consultative
- Customer-first
- Outcome-driven
- Evidence-based
- Ethical
- Repeatable

The objective is long-term customer success rather than one-time transactions.

---

# Sales Objectives

The sales organization should:

- Generate predictable revenue
- Solve customer problems
- Increase win rate
- Reduce sales cycle time
- Improve expansion revenue
- Strengthen customer relationships

---

# Revenue Execution Model

Revenue execution combines:

- Marketing
- Sales Development
- Account Executives
- Solution Engineering
- Customer Success
- Revenue Operations

Revenue is a coordinated organizational responsibility.

---

# Sales Lifecycle

Every opportunity follows a defined lifecycle.

```
Prospecting

↓

Qualification

↓

Discovery

↓

Solution Mapping

↓

Demonstration

↓

Proposal

↓

Negotiation

↓

Commitment

↓

Closed Won / Closed Lost

↓

Customer Onboarding
```

Each stage should define measurable exit criteria.

---

# Prospecting

Prospecting activities include:

- Inbound leads
- Outbound outreach
- Referrals
- Events
- Partnerships
- Community engagement

Prospecting quality is more important than prospecting volume.

---

# Qualification

Every opportunity should be qualified using a consistent framework.

Qualification should evaluate:

- Business need
- Decision authority
- Budget
- Timeline
- Strategic fit
- Technical feasibility

Unqualified opportunities should not advance.

---

# Discovery

Discovery should identify:

- Business goals
- Current challenges
- Existing processes
- Success criteria
- Risks
- Stakeholders

Discovery should prioritize listening over presenting.

---

# Solution Mapping

Every proposal should demonstrate how Avonix AI addresses the customer's specific needs.

Solution mapping should connect:

- Customer problems
- Product capabilities
- Expected outcomes
- Business value

---

# Demonstration

Product demonstrations should:

- Reflect the customer's workflow
- Focus on outcomes
- Highlight differentiators
- Avoid unnecessary feature tours

Every demo should answer the customer's most important questions.

---

# Proposal

Commercial proposals should include:

- Scope
- Deliverables
- Pricing
- Timeline
- Support
- Assumptions
- Success criteria

Proposals should remain clear and outcome-oriented.

---

# Negotiation

Negotiations should balance:

- Customer value
- Commercial sustainability
- Long-term partnership

Discounting should follow documented approval policies.

---

# Closing

Before closing, confirm:

- Commercial agreement
- Legal approval
- Technical feasibility
- Customer readiness
- Internal approvals

A successful close begins the customer relationship—it does not end it.

---

# Customer Handoff

Closed opportunities transition to Customer Success through:

- Structured handoff documentation
- Implementation plan
- Customer objectives
- Key contacts
- Success metrics

Knowledge transfer should be complete and verifiable.

---

# Pipeline Architecture

Pipeline stages should define:

- Entry criteria
- Exit criteria
- Probability
- Required activities
- Required documentation

Every opportunity must exist in one—and only one—stage.

---

# Pipeline Hygiene

The pipeline should remain:

- Current
- Accurate
- Qualified
- Forecastable
- Free of duplicates

Pipeline quality directly influences forecast quality.

---

# Forecasting

Revenue forecasts should consider:

- Stage probability
- Historical conversion
- Deal size
- Sales cycle
- Pipeline health

Forecasts should be data-informed rather than optimistic.

---

# Sales Playbooks

Playbooks should define:

- Discovery questions
- Industry guidance
- Demo strategy
- Competitive positioning
- Objection handling
- Proposal templates
- Negotiation guidelines

Playbooks ensure consistent execution.

---

# Objection Handling

Common objection categories include:

- Price
- Timing
- Competition
- Security
- Integration
- AI trust
- Implementation effort

Responses should educate rather than pressure.

---

# Competitive Positioning

Competitive discussions should emphasize:

- Customer outcomes
- Platform flexibility
- AI governance
- Total value
- Long-term scalability

Avoid negative messaging about competitors.

---

# Enterprise Sales

Enterprise opportunities may require:

- Executive sponsorship
- Multi-stakeholder engagement
- Security reviews
- Compliance reviews
- Procurement processes
- Pilot programs
- RFP/RFI responses
- Contract negotiation

Enterprise selling should remain structured and documented.

---

# Revenue Operations (RevOps)

RevOps should govern:

- CRM data quality
- Lead routing
- Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
- Territory management
- Forecasting
- Attribution
- Reporting

RevOps creates operational consistency.

---

# Sales Performance

The sales organization should monitor:

- Win rate
- Conversion rate
- Pipeline coverage
- Sales cycle length
- Average deal size
- Quota attainment
- Forecast accuracy
- Expansion revenue
- Renewal influence

Performance metrics should encourage sustainable growth.

---

# Enablement

Sales enablement should provide:

- Product training
- Competitive intelligence
- Messaging guidance
- Demo environments
- Sales certifications
- Updated documentation

Enablement is an ongoing capability.

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Advancing unqualified deals
- Inaccurate forecasts
- Inconsistent qualification
- Excessive discounting
- Poor customer handoffs
- CRM neglect

Operational discipline strengthens customer trust.

---

# Sales Review Checklist

Every opportunity should answer:

- Is the customer problem clearly understood?
- Has value been demonstrated?
- Is qualification complete?
- Are all stakeholders identified?
- Is pricing aligned with value?
- Is implementation feasible?
- Is forecasting accurate?
- Is handoff prepared?

---

# Governance

The sales program should maintain:

- Pipeline standards
- CRM governance
- Playbook library
- Forecast reviews
- Performance dashboards
- Training records
- Process ownership

Governance ensures predictable revenue execution.
