# Enterprise Governance Roles & Responsibilities Standard

> "Good governance is achieved when every decision has a clear owner, every responsibility is understood, and every authority is accountable."

---

# Purpose

This document defines the canonical Enterprise Governance Roles & Responsibilities Standard for Avonix AI.

It establishes the governance organization, decision hierarchy, accountability model, authority delegation, collaboration framework, and role lifecycle required to ensure that governance activities are executed consistently across the enterprise.

---

# Philosophy

Governance roles should be:

- Clearly defined
- Accountable
- Transparent
- Collaborative
- Business-aligned
- Risk-aware
- Continuously evolving

Authority should always be matched by responsibility and accountability.

---

# Objectives

This standard should ensure:

- Clear decision ownership
- Consistent governance execution
- Reduced ambiguity
- Effective cross-functional collaboration
- Transparent accountability
- Sustainable organizational governance

---

# Scope

Applies to:

- Executive governance
- Architecture governance
- Technology governance
- Data governance
- AI governance
- Security governance
- Risk governance
- Compliance governance
- Audit governance
- Operational governance

---

# Governance Principles

Every governance role should promote:

- Accountability
- Integrity
- Collaboration
- Independence
- Transparency
- Timely decision-making
- Organizational alignment

Governance roles should enable effective execution rather than create unnecessary bureaucracy.

---

# Governance Organizational Model

The governance structure should include:

```text
Board / Executive Leadership
            │
Enterprise Governance Council
            │
────────────────────────────────
Architecture Council
Technology Council
Data Governance Council
AI Governance Council
Security Governance Council
Risk Council
Compliance Council
Audit Council
Operations Council
```

Each council should have documented authority, scope, and reporting responsibilities.

---

# Governance Councils

Each governance council should define:

- Purpose
- Scope
- Membership
- Decision authority
- Meeting cadence
- Reporting obligations
- Escalation responsibilities

Council charters should remain documented and periodically reviewed.

---

# Executive Sponsor

Executive Sponsors should provide:

- Strategic direction
- Funding support
- Governance endorsement
- Cross-functional alignment
- Escalation resolution
- Final approval for enterprise-level decisions

Executive Sponsors remain accountable for governance effectiveness within their domain.

---

# Governance Council Responsibilities

Governance Councils should oversee:

- Policy approval
- Standard ownership
- Strategic alignment
- Cross-domain coordination
- Risk oversight
- Performance reviews
- Continuous improvement

Councils should focus on governance rather than operational execution.

---

# Domain Role Responsibilities

Each governance domain should maintain clearly defined responsibilities for:

- Architecture
- Technology
- Data
- AI
- Security
- Risk
- Compliance
- Audit
- Operations

Responsibilities should avoid overlap while encouraging collaboration.

---

# Decision Authority

Decision authority should define:

- Enterprise-level decisions
- Domain-level decisions
- Delegated authority
- Escalation thresholds
- Approval requirements

Authority should be proportionate to organizational impact.

---

# Delegation of Authority

Delegated authority should document:

- Delegated responsibilities
- Scope limitations
- Approval boundaries
- Review requirements
- Revocation process

Delegation should preserve accountability while enabling efficient decision-making.

---

# RACI Governance Model

Governance activities should define:

- Responsible
- Accountable
- Consulted
- Informed

The RACI model should be maintained for major governance processes to reduce ambiguity and improve coordination.

---

# Cross-Functional Collaboration

Governance should encourage collaboration through:

- Joint reviews
- Shared decision forums
- Cross-domain working groups
- Escalation pathways
- Knowledge sharing

Collaboration should balance domain expertise with enterprise consistency.

---

# Conflict Resolution

Governance conflicts should follow:

```text
Working Team
      ↓
Domain Council
      ↓
Enterprise Governance Council
      ↓
Executive Sponsor
```

Conflict resolution should be timely, documented, and transparent.

---

# Governance Communication

Governance communication should define:

- Meeting cadence
- Decision records
- Reporting channels
- Stakeholder updates
- Escalation notices

Communication should ensure stakeholders remain informed of governance outcomes.

---

# Role Lifecycle

Every governance role should progress through:

```text
Appointment
      ↓
Orientation
      ↓
Active Service
      ↓
Periodic Review
      ↓
Succession
      ↓
Retirement
```

Role transitions should preserve governance continuity.

---

# Competency Expectations

Governance participants should demonstrate:

- Domain expertise
- Strategic thinking
- Risk awareness
- Communication skills
- Decision-making ability
- Ethical judgment

Competency expectations should support effective governance outcomes.

---

# Documentation

Governance documentation should maintain:

- Council charters
- Role descriptions
- Delegation records
- Decision records
- Meeting minutes
- RACI matrices
- Succession plans

Documentation should remain current, accessible, and audit-ready.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Governance roles should improve through:

- Performance reviews
- Governance assessments
- Stakeholder feedback
- Organizational changes
- Lessons learned
- Leadership development

Continuous improvement should strengthen governance maturity over time.

---

# Governance

This standard requires:

- Enterprise Governance Council approval
- Executive Sponsor endorsement
- Cross-domain council review
- Periodic governance assessment

Governance structures should evolve alongside organizational growth and strategic priorities.

---

# Success Metrics

Governance role effectiveness may be evaluated through:

- Decision turnaround time
- Governance participation rate
- RACI coverage
- Escalation resolution time
- Cross-functional collaboration effectiveness
- Governance review completion
- Stakeholder satisfaction
